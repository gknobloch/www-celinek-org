# PRODUCT — Céline Knobloch, Home Organiser (redesign target)

## Register
`brand` — personal-service marketing. Warm, human, one conversion path (contact).

## Product Purpose
Convert Haut-Rhin visitors into a first contact with Céline Knobloch, a
professional home organiser ("coach en rangement"), for an at-home
decluttering-and-organising engagement. Explain what home organising is, why to
hire a coach, how the process works, and prove it with client stories.

## Users
Individuals — predominantly women, per the captured voice — overwhelmed by
clutter, for whom it carries emotional weight, who want a calmer home and can't
start or sustain it alone. Local prospects willing to host an in-home visit.

## Brand Personality
Warm, gentle, reassuring, human, unhurried. Care- and wellness-adjacent (Céline
is a former nurse — douceur, bienveillance, écoute, sérénité). On-page values:
respect, écoute, authenticité. Non-judgmental and intimate. Soft rounded
geometry + a hand-scripted voice.

## Anti-references
Corporate/clinical productivity tone; loud high-density SaaS marketing; generic
stock-photo wellness. The one real portrait is the brand's strongest asset.

## Design Principles
1. Calm cream ground; color as warmth, never alarm.
2. Rounded, soft forms.
3. One human face carries trust.
4. Reassurance over feature density.
5. A single, low-friction conversion path (contact).
