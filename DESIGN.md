# DESIGN — Céline Knobloch (redesign target, Mode A)

Palette and type are **pinned** to the captured brand surface. The redesign
moves composition, density, hierarchy, and (for variant C) motion — not the
brand's colors or fonts.

## Palette
Two-hue warm system on cream. Sage `#728e7f` is primary/interactive; gold
`#eed366` is warmth — now **promoted to the conversion CTA** so the contact
action is the brightest thing on the page. Soft gold `#f8e1af` fills calm bands.
Darker sage/gold variants exist only to hit AA on small text/borders.

## Typography
Comfortaa (rounded geometric sans) for body + headings. Sacramento (script) is
the signature voice — restrained in A, structural in B, cinematic-led in C.
Type ratio ≥ 1.25 maintained for the brand register.

## Layout & motifs
Editorial, breathing whitespace, pill buttons, warm tinted bands, one hero
portrait reused across sections. Numbered 3-step process. Testimonials shown
without hiding behind arrows.
