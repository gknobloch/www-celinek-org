# PRODUCT — Céline Knobloch, Home Organiser (current state)

## Register
`brand` — a personal-service marketing site for a solo practitioner. Warm, human, conversion toward one contact action.

## Product Purpose
Present Céline Knobloch, a professional *home organiser* / "coach en rangement" serving the Haut-Rhin (France), and convert visitors into a first contact (email/phone) for an at-home decluttering-and-organising engagement. The page explains what home organising is, why to hire a coach, how the process works, and offers social proof.

## Users
- Individuals (predominantly, from the voice, women) who feel overwhelmed by clutter and want a calmer home but can't get started or sustain it alone.
- People for whom clutter carries emotional weight — the copy explicitly names "blocages psychologiques" and objects with "poids émotionnel."
- Local prospects in the Haut-Rhin who will host an in-home visit.

## Brand Personality
Warm, gentle, reassuring, human. Wellness- and care-adjacent (Céline is a former nurse — "infirmière" — and leans on douceur, bienveillance, écoute, sérénité). Values stated on-page: **respect, écoute, authenticité**. The register is intimate and non-judgmental ("Céline ne juge personne"), calm rather than energetic. Soft rounded geometry (Comfortaa + pill buttons) and a hand-scripted tagline reinforce a personal, artisanal, unhurried feeling.

_Brand-Personality → motion-register signal: the dominant traits are calm, warm, editorial-human, unhurried — a **quiet arrival / editorial** register, not kinetic or data-driven._

## Anti-references
- Corporate/clinical productivity or "life-hack efficiency" tone — the brand is emotional and human, not a checklist.
- Loud, high-contrast, high-density SaaS marketing.
- Generic stock-photo wellness look — the site's one real portrait is its strongest asset.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. Calm cream ground; color used sparingly as warmth (sage green + gold), never as alarm.
2. Rounded, soft forms (Comfortaa, pill buttons).
3. One human face carries trust.
4. Long-form reassurance copy over dense feature grids.
5. A single, low-friction conversion path (contact).
