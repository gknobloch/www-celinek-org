`#4a6357`, gold `#eed366`, warm band `#f8e1af`, sage band `#e4ebe6`, contact

(Accueil · Qui suis-je ? · Prestations et Tarifs · Mes Réalisations · gold
"Contactez-moi"), condensing header, gold contact band, deep-sage footer.

(Cellier 2 · Cuisine 5 · Dressing 4 · Chambre 1 · Salle de bain 3 · Atelier 3 ·

## Variant A — Faithful + improvements
Role: green-light. "The page you have, in the new design, fixed."
Composition: same IA — page head (script + h1 + intro) → rooms → pairs → contact.
Improvements applied: 1–7 (canon chrome, room chapters, room index chips, a11y
labels + alt, responsive comparison slider, contact band, og:image note).
Motion: static page content (site chrome only: condensing nav).

## Variant B — What if visitors started from their own room?
Role: design-team motivator. Catalog #7 Audience-routing reframe (+ derived D1).
Evidence: 9 rooms / 14 headings; "la/les pièce(s) concernée(s)" (home, step 2).
Composition: filter bar ("Toutes · Cuisine 5 · Dressing 4 …") driving an
après-first photo grid; each card flips to its *avant* on demand and opens a
large comparison in a dialog; two real review excerpts as interludes (never
attributed to a room). Static.

## Variant C — What if the scroll turned "avant" into "après"?
Role: visionary pitch. Catalog #2 Photography re-foregrounding, register
**editorial** (inherited from the homepage canon — DESIGN-C.json).
Composition: same IA as A; the bet is motion. Full-bleed hero (chambres d'amis
1) whose *avant* dissolves into *après* as you scroll; each room chapter opens
on a pinned stage where a soft feathered wipe carries the lead pair from
avant to après; remaining pairs reveal with the canon fade-rise + photo drift;
sticky room rail marks the current chapter.
Refuses: hard clip reveals (wipe is feathered 16%), count-ups, tickers.
Reduced motion: stages render side-by-side avant/après, no pinning.

## Differentiation
- A vs B: B replaces the linear room chapters with filter routing + après-first cards + dialog + interludes (≥ 3 structural changes).
- A vs C: C adds the photo hero, pinned dissolve stages, room rail (≥ 3).
- B vs C: routing/IA (static) vs. motion/photography (same IA as A) — different traits, different axes.
