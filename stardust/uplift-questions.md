# "What if…" candidates — https://www.celinek.org

Cinematic register (Phase 3a): **editorial** — brand personality is slow-paced,
warm, care-led, with one strong identity photograph and long-form reassurance
copy (the publication register per motion-registers.md § Selection heuristic:
`editorial` / `slow-paced`). Runner-up: `arrival` (calm brand reveal) — set
aside because the captured photography + long-form voice pull editorial.

## Picked

### Variant C · Photography re-foregrounding
Source: catalog (#2)
What if: "What if Céline's portrait breathed at editorial scale, and the whole page were built around her presence instead of around text?"
Cinematic register: editorial
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json.photography` — a single identity-quality 2000w
environmental portrait (`hero-celine.webp`), cropped once into a wide hero band
and never reused (tension T2). Register-match: editorial's hero-photo
micro-parallax (8–12vh) and slow reveals naturally amplify a photography-led
brand.
Motion bet: the portrait leads; soft micro-parallax on the hero photo, slow
fade-and-rise reveals on editorial photo/text blocks as they enter, no counters
or tickers (editorial doesn't count its readers).

### Variant B · Display-typography amplification
Source: catalog (#1)
What if: "What if Sacramento stopped being a one-line flourish and became the page's structural voice?"
Captured trait amplified: the signature script (Sacramento)
Evidence: `_brand-extraction.json.type.families.display` — Sacramento, a
distinctive single-weight cursive, with `usageCount: 1` (hero tagline only),
while Comfortaa carries both headings and body so the type system has no display
contrast (tension T1).
Composition bet: promote Sacramento into section openers and pull-quotes as a
recurring editorial device, restructure the page around scripted section
headers + a scannable value grid, and lift the warm-gold accent to carry those
moments. Axis = typography/composition (differentiates from C's photography and
A's IA fixes).

## Disqualified

- **Live-data promotion (#3)** — disqualified: a solo personal-service marketing
  site exposes no live/operational data (no wait times, inventory, status). No
  liveness to promote.
- **Audience-routing reframe (#7)** — disqualified: the page serves a single
  clear audience (individuals overwhelmed by clutter); the above-the-fold region
  has fewer than 4 distinct CTA verbs. No multi-audience compression to untangle.

## Considered but not picked

- **Voice-register pivot (#5)** — real tonal contrast exists (warm/caring
  dominant vs the method/process tone in "Comment ça se passe ?"). Not picked:
  it maps to the same `editorial` register as C, and B differentiates better by
  taking the typography axis. The method tone is instead surfaced via A's
  numbered-process improvement (#4).
- **Signature-gesture extension (#4)** — partial trigger (mascot logo + script
  tagline appear only at the header/hero). Not picked: the mascot alone is a thin
  gesture, and B's display-typography bet captures the "extend the signature into
  a system" energy through the script, which carries more of the brand's voice.
- **Color-ladder re-weighting (#6)** — real trigger (vivid gold `#eed366` reserved
  to link-hover; soft gold `#f8e1af` used as bands). Not picked as a standalone
  variant: folded into A's improvement #5 (promote gold to the conversion CTA) and
  reinforced inside B's scripted-accent composition.
- **Motif vocabulary swap (#8)** — weak trigger: the page leans on one dominant
  primitive (full-bleed tinted bands + prose); the strongest alternate (the
  3-step method as an indexed list) is captured by A's improvement #4. Too thin
  to carry a variant.
